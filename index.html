Chapter I

Return by Death

How dying works, what it costs, and what you keep.

Each character has 4 lives. Outside the story, a combat death costs one life (and in multiplayer a quarter of your gold, per the notes). Your fourth death condemns the character, who is wiped and sent to Satella's Realm; there a one-time restoration (a paid restoration; free for a Shop Token holder) gives back 1 life. After Arc 1, a mind broken to 0 Sanity condemns the character like a fourth death (in Arc 1 Sanity never drops below 1). Return by Death is a Mythical card (from level 2): with it a death costs no life and rewinds you to your last checkpoint, losing progress, XP, gold, items and claims since then. Checkpoints are taken at most every 10 minutes and at boss defeats. In the first-arc story, deaths are free: you rewind without losing a life or consuming the card (the story keeps a stall checkpoint and a soft checkpoint after about 15 active minutes in safe hubs; the opening's first two alley losses are rescues, not deaths). Story knowledge survives every rewind. In Arc 2, each story death costs one life (the card holder pays none); in Arc 3 the scripted deaths are free. In Solo mode combat deaths are free and rewind to a solo checkpoint, but confessing Return by Death spends a solo life. Defenders killed in PvP and silent (suspended) clients spend no life.

Four lives

Every character has four lives. The fourth death condemns them to Satella's Realm.

The first arc is forgiving

Story deaths in Arc 1 rewind you without costing a life. What you learned survives every rewind.

Return by Death card

A Mythical card. While you hold it, a death costs no life and rewinds you to your last checkpoint instead.

Chapter II

Story & Routes

Every arc in order, every road the story can split into, and exactly what you have to do to walk it.

The arcs

Arc 1: The Capital (modern opening, four loops of one day)

You arrive in the capital's market, win or lose a trial against three alley bandits (Emilia helps), search for the thief with her, can hunt the Man in the Hat, the Mutated Mabeast and Sir Baldric, then live four loops of one day that end at the loot house with Elsa. The arc ends with a choice between the Main and Pride routes.

How it plays

  • Opening: Market, then the alley trial (Ton, Kan, Chin). Attempts 1-2: a loss ends in Emilia's rescue. Attempt 3 onward: a loss is a Return by Death. After the meeting with the silver-haired girl (Emilia) comes the market leads (any one of: the lost child, the pickpocket chase, the lost delivery), the shops, and the sealed East Gate with its knight.
  • The optional hunt (any free stage): the knight's warning about the bounty, one clue from four witnesses (Lamplighter, Laundress, Cart Driver, Pawnbroker), Wren at her spot, her deal (lift her travel ban, no gold), Tomas Reed's terms (kill the forest boss), Emilia's briefing, the hunt, the kill and proof, then Wren's escort to the Man in the Hat and the red tassel.
  • Loop 1: Market, then the shops, the spring (a spirit in the water), the slums and the dark loot house. It ends there; you wake on the stall.
  • Loop 2: Market, slums, loot house, Elsa alone. It ends; you wake on the stall. This teaches the killer, the buyer, the password, Felt the thief and Rom the appraiser.
  • Loop 3: Market, then the alley: the taboo, the snatch and the knife. It ends with the knife death, or a HELP call skips straight to loop 4. Winning the alley alone is possible and keeps Pride open.
  • Loop 4: Market, alley, slums, loot house, Elsa (hold 105 seconds, reinforcements arrive), then the Resolve choice and the ending. The ending is 'The Road to the Mansion'.
  • Level cap: 15 during Arc 1's story, rising to 25 once the story is Complete.

Arc 2: Roswaal Mansion (Main track)

After Arc 1 Main you ride to the Roswaal Mansion in a private server. You start in an endless corridor, wake at the save point, and live one week (five days). Your Return by Death replays the same week, and what you learn carries over while the world resets. The week covers onboarding on day 1 (Roswaal's return, the Hired or Guest choice, uniform, tour, chores), Beatrice's library, the bite and curse, the village trip to Arlam on day 3, the barrier, the children, the Great Ulgarm, Oni Rem and the Pack Leader, then Roswaal's garden. The arc ends with the capital calling and a ride home.

How it plays

  • Loop 0: the corridor, no Return by Death yet.
  • Later loops wake at the save point with 'nobody remembers' instead of the first arrival. The corridor never replays.
  • Rem's suspicion and trust are hidden. Suspicion starts at 20 plus 10 per rewind (capped at 50) and drops 10 at the dawns of days 2, 3 and 4. The only warning is the chain sound.
  • Chapters in order: The Endless Corridor, A Place at the Estate, Earning Your Keep, The Road to Arlam, The Children of Arlam, After the Barrier, The Road Back; plus the alternate chapter Words You Cannot Say if you take the isolation path.
  • Failure endings: sleeping through night 4 with an unbroken curse, Rem hunting a hostile host in the house or outside the grounds, the cliff, a fight loss. Each failure is a Return by Death.
  • The Isolation path (push Emilia away): if you know the curse spreads or kills casters, Beatrice catches the curse and the run continues. If not, Rem dies and the cliff scene opens, where the Wrath route is offered.
  • The arc requires three bosses in order: Great Ulgarm, Oni Rem and the Pack Leader. A Guest can leave after day 2 only while Rem is Hostile.

Arc 2 for Pride: Following the Witch Cult

A Pride character skips the mansion. At the East Gate a prompt 'Follow the cultists' leads to the cult's cave in the woods of Emilia's domain: nine scenes (a cave wake, the lair, Elsa and Meili's lodge, the ruined Arlam graves, the lodge again, the cave, the trial clearing, the nook, and the cave mouth), with no fights, deaths or loops. The Pride card shows at the end.

How it plays

  • No loops: nine fixed beats, each a walk to a place and its scene.

Arc 3: The Royal Selection and the Road (Main track)

A character who finishes Arc 2 arrives for the Royal Selection (East Gate, castle, great hall, quarrel on the terrace) and then plays five chapters: The Karsten House, The Long Road Home, Nobody Comes, The Canal Stair and The Knight. The story has Return-by-Death style loops with choices, three required bosses (White Whale, a Finger of Sloth, Petelgeuse) and ends with 'Arc 3: Complete'.

How it plays

  • Chapter 1 ends in a death at Arlam; chapter 2 ends in Rem's death in the forest; chapter 3 ends in Arlam's fall; chapter 4 ends in a death at Arlam. Each wake is a scripted Return by Death at a save beat (free of lives).
  • Wrong answers at some choices replay the scene and ask again, or are fatal (a scripted Return by Death, then asked again).
  • Choices that matter: whether you accept Rem's help (ride on or rest), whether you read her letter, whether you tell Emilia the truth, what you offer Crusch (Russell's deal, then the whale's hour and place), which dragon (the gentle one or the black one), how you lure the whale (alone with your scent, never wait), who you trust against Sloth (Julius, never alone), the schedule you give the merchant spy, and whether you say the forbidden words to purge Petelgeuse.
  • The three bosses must fall in order for the arc to complete.

  2. Choose to turn Elsa away (anything other than Recruit), or fail the Pride conditions.

Arc 1 Pride route

Playable

How to get it

  1. Never call HELP in the alley encounters (an answered call permanently removes Pride).
  2. Beat the alley trio (Ton, Kan, Chin) without help in the current timeline.
  3. Die at least once (have used Return by Death).
  4. Tell the wall guards about the loot house, or bribe the cordon guard 100 gold at the closed South Gate (offered only if you also won loop 3's alley alone and never called help) so the guards come first at the loot house.
  5. Hold Elsa until the clock is full, with the guards as the first reinforcement.
  6. Then choose 'Recruit Elsa' in the loot-house recruitment scene or at the Resolve choice.

What changes

You get the Pride route card (Subaru Pride) and Elsa becomes your companion from the Resolve stage. Pride skips the Roswaal Mansion.

How it ends

Arc 1 Complete. Once Pride Arc 2 opens, an East Gate prompt 'Follow the cultists' leads to the cult cave (a party host's party rides together). A Pride character is not owed Arc 2 Main or the Arc 3 Main story.

Seven Sins fate campaign (Savior route and seven sin routes)

Not open yet

How to get it

  1. This is a separate data-complete campaign that starts with Chapter 1 'The Seventh Bell', locked at a gate by your leanings and seeds.
  2. Characters who start in the modern opening never enter this campaign; it stays 'Unwritten'.

Chapter III

Races & Subspecies

Every race you can be born as, how rare it is, what it gives you, and every bloodline inside it.

A new character rolls a race on the server. One draw first checks two special bloodlines: a hidden secret one (0.095% of all rolls; its name is never shown, so it is left out of this sheet) and Van Astrea, the Sword Saint's bloodline (0.045% of all rolls, named on screen). Otherwise a rarity pool is picked: Common 50%, Uncommon 30%, Rare 15%, Epic 4%, Legendary 0.8%, Mythical 0.2% (shares of the 99.86% left after the specials), then each race in the pool is equally likely. Pools: Common = Human, Goblin; Uncommon = Kararagian, Dog-folk, Emberi, Orc, Dwarf; Rare = Vollachian, Half-Elf, Tideborn, Elf, Minotaur; Epic = Cat-folk, Fire Spirit-born, Ice Spirit-born, Tinsel; Legendary = Dragonkin, Holy Spirit-born, Demon, Oni; Mythical = Great Spirit-born. Subspecies: the server rolls one subspecies from the race's own table at the shown odds; subspecies are cosmetic only (no stats or bonuses). Rerolls cost a Race Reroll ticket; a subspecies reroll never repeats the current one and the other odds keep their relative shares. The first roll also costs a ticket (a new character starts with one ticket that can be used twice). A finished character can reroll its race with a book item; level, name, inventory, stats and protections are kept. Characters saved before subspecies existed have none and keep full appearance freedom. Griffin Hybrid is acquired by eating a Griffin Fruit, is soul-bound, and cannot be chosen or rolled. Beastman, Cyborg, Three Eyes and Giant are registered but not yet in the roll pools.

Chapter IV

Divine Protections

All 105 blessings you can roll, how rare each one is, and what it does.

Divine Protections are obtained only by rolling in the Divine Protection book UI (no cards, direct rewards, automatic boss rolls or lineage grants). A roll uses one Divine Protection roll credit per character. New characters start with zero credits and nothing equipped. Credits come from: the first-time win over each major story boss (1 free reroll per boss kill per character, Elsa first); Divine Protection Ticket bag items (redeeming one adds one roll credit for that character); and the Shop Token (holder rolls for free, for life, with unlimited rolls). Paid rolls are not on sale. Odds: a tier is picked by tier percentage, then every protection in that tier is equally likely; no pity system. A rolled result is then resolved: Keep (dispose it), Store, Replace, or Equip straight into an open slot (the displaced protection goes to storage). Storage is effectively unlimited (free storage 1,000,000). You cannot store a duplicate of a protection you already hold; only one copy of any protection may be equipped. Equip slots: none until you beat Elsa (arc 1 boss); then 1 slot, more as later story arcs complete, maximum 9. The Van Astrea lineage has 9 slots. Storage, bought storage and unspent credits stay with the character slot after a wipe. Default ability key is Y (rebindable); on controller/touch the Divine Protection action and a slot-switch action sit in the action wheel. Most protections show exact numbers only through their description; cooldown values are in seconds. Authorities and true damage bypass ordinary protections.

Arc 1
Where
The abandoned white house, the left-hand door among the three capital boss houses. Inside lies the murdered Duke Rowan Lugunica.
Level
13 (fixed, never scales to you). You need level 5 to enter.

  • Three phases: the phase changes come at roughly two thirds and one third of his health
  • Moonlit riposte: if you parry him he blinks behind you and cuts

  • Severing draw: one red line straight through you; sidestep it, guarding fails
  • Three crossings (phase 2+): three red lines through where you stand
  • Witch halo (phase 3): a room-wide ring attack with only the ground at his feet safe, then his feet
  • He also leans on his katana arts depending on whether you guard, dodge or rush

  • First credited win: the hat (quest proof for the East Gate bounty)

  • Hooded Candidate's Robe (Legendary cape) 0.4%
